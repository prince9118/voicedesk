# Resetting your password

## Requesting a reset link
Go to the VoiceDesk sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter the email address on your account and we will send a reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once.

## The reset email did not arrive
Reset emails usually arrive within two minutes. Check your spam or junk folder and search for messages from no-reply@voicedesk.example. Make sure you entered the same email address you registered with; for privacy, we do not tell you whether an address has an account. If nothing arrives after 15 minutes, request a new link. Requesting a new link cancels the previous one.

## Choosing a new password
Passwords must be at least 12 characters. We recommend a passphrase of several unrelated words. Changing your password signs you out on all other devices.
