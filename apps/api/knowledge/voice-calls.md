# Voice support calls

## Starting a voice call
Sign in, open Support, and choose "Talk to the assistant". Your browser will ask for microphone permission; choose Allow. The assistant can check the status of your tickets and create new tickets for you.

## The assistant cannot hear me
Check that your browser has microphone permission for VoiceDesk and that the correct microphone is selected in your system settings. Close other apps that may be using the microphone, such as video calling apps. Headphones reduce echo and help the assistant hear you clearly.

## Call quality and recording
Voice calls work best on a stable connection; mobile data and busy Wi-Fi can cause gaps. Calls are not recorded by default.
