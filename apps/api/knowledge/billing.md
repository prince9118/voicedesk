# Billing and invoices

## Downloading invoices
Invoices are available under Settings, then Billing, then Invoice history. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. Invoices are issued on the day you are charged.

## Updating your payment method
Go to Settings, then Billing, then Payment method, and add a new card. The new card is used from the next charge onwards. We never store your full card number; it is held by our payment provider.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
A pending authorisation can appear for a few days and then disappear; it is not a charge. If you see two completed charges for the same period, open a support ticket with both dates and amounts and we will refund the duplicate.
