# Refunds

## Refund eligibility
You can request a refund within 30 days of a charge if you have not used the paid features in that billing period. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 60 days. Charges older than that are not refundable, but you can cancel to stop future charges.

## How long a refund takes
Once a refund is approved, we send it to your original payment method the same day. Banks and card issuers usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement. Refunds to PayPal usually appear within 3 business days.

## Refund not received
If more than 10 business days have passed since approval, first check the statement for the card you originally paid with, because refunds cannot be sent to a different card. If it is still missing, open a support ticket with the approximate charge date and the last four digits of the card, and we will send you the refund reference number your bank can trace.
